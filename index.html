Topics Covered

All seven chapters of the CISI IBOC Operational Risk workbook (Edition 26)

Ch 1: Risk Basics

Risk definitions, ERM, risk appetite/capacity/tolerance, major incidents (LIBOR, Wells Fargo, TSB, Barings, SocGen)

Ch 2: Other Major Risks

Credit risk (CDS, DVP, netting, ratings, LGD/PD), Market risk (VaR, back-testing, stress testing), Liquidity risk (LCR, NSFR)

Ch 3: Nature of Operational Risk

BIS definition, Basel II event types, three lines of defence, RCSA, KRIs, scenario analysis, benchmarking, risk appetite statements

Ch 4: Causes & Impacts of OR

People risk, process risk, systems/cyber risk (DDoS, phishing, ransomware), external events, BCP/DRP, outsourcing risk

Ch 5: Operational Risks in the Trade Cycle

Pre-trade, execution, confirmation, clearing, settlement (DVP), corporate actions, KYC/AML, suitability (MiFID II)

Ch 6: Support & Control Functions

Front/middle/back office, compliance, legal (contract formation), HR (TUPE, key person), finance/accounting risk, project management

Ch 7: Regulatory Environment

Basel II/III (Pillars 1–3, BIA/TSA/AMA), FCA/PRA dual regulation, SMCR, EMIR, GDPR, Operational Resilience framework
